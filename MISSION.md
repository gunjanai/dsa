# Mission: Mastering DSA Patterns

## Why
To build deep, intuitive problem-solving fluency in Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) by mastering one pattern at a time. The ultimate goal is to confidently identify and solve "Hard" complexity interview problems in target companies under timed pressure, transforming abstract pattern recognition into permanent muscle memory.

## Success looks like
- Concurrently identifying the correct pattern for any unseen LeetCode-style problem within 5 minutes.
- Solving "Hard" level questions for a pattern with optimal time and space complexity without looking at hints.
- Explaining the underlying logic and complexity trade-offs of solutions with high pedagogical clarity.

## Constraints
- **Self-Paced Iteration:** We will not move on from a pattern until confidence and mastery on "Hard" problems are fully demonstrated.
- **Immediate Practice:** Each theoretical lesson must be grounded in immediate interactive or coding-based execution.

## Out of scope
- Theoretical/mathematical proofs that don't directly assist in identifying, designing, or implementing optimal solutions.
- Rushing through patterns for the sake of ticking off boxes; depth and retention take absolute precedence over speed.
